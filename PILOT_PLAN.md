### Group 3 — Media & Knowledge (3 channels)

12. **Science Facts** — educational content

### Group 4 — Industry & News (2 channels)

## Publishing Mode Distribution
- **Auto (3 channels):** Anime News Daily, Tech News Today, Entertainment Memes
- **Approval Required (10 channels):** Manga, Gaming, Movies, AI, Space, Science, Auto, Новости
- **Manual (1 channel):** AI Media Factory (VK)

## Cron Schedules
- **Every 6 hours (4/day):** Anime News, Gaming, AI News, Tech News
- **Every 8 hours (3/day):** Manga, Movies, Auto
- **Every 12 hours (2/day):** Space, Science
- **Once per day:** Memes
- **Every hour:** Новости 📰, AI Media Factory (legacy)

## Success Criteria
- Publish success rate > 95%
- Approval rate > 70%
- Pipeline failures < 5%
- Media success rate > 90%
