# AI Media Factory

# Business Rules

Version: 1.0

Status: Active Development


---

# 1. Business Overview


AI Media Factory is an autonomous media production platform.


The system manages multiple Telegram channels using AI agents.


Main objective:


Create, grow and optimize digital media channels with minimal human involvement.



---

# 2. Media Network Concept


The platform operates as a network of independent Telegram channels.



Example:



AI Media Factory

↓

Channel 1: Artificial Intelligence

↓

Channel 2: Technology News

↓

Channel 3: Finance

↓

Channel 4: Business

↓

Channel 5: Science

↓

Channel 6: Gaming

↓

Channel 7: Education

↓

Channel 8: Productivity

↓

Channel 9: World News

↓

Channel 10: Specialized niche channel



Each channel has:


- own topic
- own audience
- own style
- own publishing strategy
- own analytics



---

# 3. Channel Rules


Every Telegram channel must have:



## Identity


Required:


- name
- category
- target audience
- language
- style profile



## Content Strategy


Each channel defines:


- preferred topics
- forbidden topics
- tone of voice
- publishing frequency



---

# 4. Content Lifecycle


Every content item follows:



Research


↓

Topic Evaluation


↓

Brief Generation


↓

Draft Generation


↓

Quality Review


↓

Image Generation


↓

Publishing


↓

Analytics


↓

Optimization



No stage should be skipped.



---

# 5. Topic Selection Rules


Topics are selected based on:



## Score


Range:


0-10



Calculation factors:


- relevance
- popularity
- novelty
- source reliability
- audience interest



---

# 6. Priority Rules


High priority:



score >= 8



Action:


Generate content immediately.



---


Medium priority:



score 5-7.9



Action:


Store for review or scheduled publishing.



---


Low priority:



score < 5



Action:


Reject or archive.



---

# 7. Content Quality Rules


Every generated post must satisfy:



## Accuracy


Content must:


- use reliable sources
- avoid fabricated facts
- preserve context



## Readability


Content must:


- be understandable
- avoid unnecessary complexity
- match audience level



## Originality


Content should:


- not copy source articles
- provide additional value



---

# 8. Telegram Publishing Rules


Default Telegram post format:



Title


↓

Main text


↓

Optional links


↓

Image



Requirements:



Maximum length:


1024 characters



Media:


1 image per post



Links:


1-2 relevant links



---

# 9. Channel Style Rules


AI must adapt every publication to channel identity.



Style parameters:



- vocabulary
- sentence length
- formatting
- emotional tone
- use of emojis
- technical depth



Example:



Professional channel:


No excessive emojis.

Analytical style.



Entertainment channel:


More emotional style.

Short sentences.



---

# 10. Publishing Frequency


Frequency is controlled per channel.



Examples:



News channel:


5-10 posts/day



Professional channel:


1-3 posts/day



Niche channel:


1 post/day



Frequency must be optimized using analytics.



---

# 11. Human Approval Mode


The system supports two modes.



## Autonomous Mode


AI publishes automatically.



## Approval Mode


AI prepares content.

Human approves before publication.



---

# 12. Analytics Rules


Every published post must collect:



- views
- reactions
- shares
- comments
- engagement rate



Analytics are used to improve future content.



---

# 13. Optimization Rules


The system must analyze:



Successful posts:


- topic
- length
- style
- publication time
- image type



Failed posts:


- low engagement
- weak topics
- incorrect timing



Results are used by Recommendation Engine.
